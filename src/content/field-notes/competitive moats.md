High conviction is necessary to build a company. High conviction should stem from strategic defensibility, otherwise known as a competitive moat. The survival of a business depends on its ability to defend its market share:

1. Your business has a first-mover advantage, creates network effects, and can sustain the network effect through stickiness
2. Your business outcompetes your competitors in a manner where they are disincentivized to devote resources to copy you
3. Your business has a competitive advantage that creates the majority of your business value and is impossible to copy (e.g. intangible assets such as a patent, physical asset such as unique location, intellectual assets such as specialized and experienced people, etc.)
