I grew up living in eight different countries: India, Nepal, Japan, Ukraine, Uganda, China, Rwanda, and Canada. It wasn't easy having to move every three years of my life but as a child, it wasn't as hard as people would normally think. This is because I have been blessed in this life with a loving, caring, and truly special family. My parents did all of the hard things to make my life and my brother's life feel easy.

Sure, I had to make new friends and adjust to different education systems but I also had to travel to countries most people would never think of visiting and reinvent myself. Looking back, I believe there are three learnings that I think deeply shape me today.

## Optimism and kindness as a default mindset

I went to a Japanese pre-school where the main language of communication was Japanese. None of my teachers and other students knew English, and I didn't know Japanese. I was the only Indian kid in my class and it was made clear to me that I was different.
